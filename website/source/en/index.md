# Small apps, made yours.

Panda Drawer helps you find and use small personal apps. When something does not work the way you want, give the app's project folder to your own AI tool and ask it to change it.

**Choose an app → Use it → Ask your AI to make it yours.**

Your apps and their data stay on your computer. Panda Drawer does not include an AI service; you choose the external AI tools and any online services your apps use.

## What you can do

- Add an app from the recommendations, a public GitHub or Gitee link, or a folder on your computer.
- Fill in its settings, open it in its own window, and keep using it as a personal tool.
- Create a starter app and ask your AI to build what you need.
- Give an existing app to your AI with its actual project location and the platform instructions.

The platform supports Chinese and English. Apps manage their own language independently, so an app can follow your system or offer its own language setting.

## Availability and downloads

**Panda Drawer is currently an internal development version. Public installers are not available yet.** If someone provided you with a development build, ask that person for the correct package and setup instructions.

This English page is an introduction, not a public download release. The guides and app pages linked below are currently in Chinese.

## System requirements

| System | Current development target |
| --- | --- |
| macOS | macOS 15 or later, Apple Silicon; an APFS workspace on a local or directly attached disk |
| Windows | Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, x64, WebView2 Evergreen; a workspace on a local internal NTFS disk |

Some features are currently available only on macOS, including workspace relocation, AI tool detection and one-click handoff, and app title-bar actions. Windows supports copying handoff instructions and opening the project folder. Support targets do not mean every device and usage scenario has been tested.
