## For AI tools and app authors

The prompt Panda Drawer generates already includes the location of the platform instructions on your computer. Use these pages to look up requirements and formats.

- [Platform rules](/en/wiki/platform-rules/) · How AI should inspect and change an app.
- [panda.json reference](/en/wiki/panda-json/) · Fields, paths, configuration declarations, and complete examples.
- [App types and runtime](/en/wiki/app-types/) · Web pages, Node, the SDK, the control protocol, and app windows.
- [Data, configuration, logs, and deletion](/en/wiki/data-and-config/) · Storage locations and what changes when you delete an app.
- [Ways to add an app](/en/wiki/adding-apps/) · Add from a link or folder, create a starter app, or assess an existing one.

[llms.txt](/llms.txt) lists the full addresses of these pages. The [platform instructions](https://github.com/happypandaa/panda-drawer/blob/develop/skills/local-projects-app/SKILL.md) are maintained alongside the platform. Follow the copy installed on your computer.
