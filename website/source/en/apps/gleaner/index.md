# Gleaner

Write down the topics you care about. Your own AI tool gathers and organizes the news, and you read the briefing in Gleaner. Bookmarks, notes, and feedback stay on your computer so the AI can use them in the next update.

App links [GitHub](https://github.com/happypandaa/gleaner.git) · [Gitee](https://gitee.com/mycolorfuldays/gleaner.git)

## Get started

1. In Panda Drawer, find Gleaner under **Recommended apps**, click **Add**, and choose where to put it. Open Gleaner when the download finishes.
2. Use **交给我的 AI** (hand off to my AI) and review the prepared message in your AI tool before sending it. If Gleaner only offers **复制** (copy), copy the message, open the Gleaner project folder in your AI tool, and send it there.
3. Tell your AI what to follow. For example, ask for up to five updates per edition on new features in independent software, with priority given to the authors' release notes. Once you confirm the topic, the AI prepares the first edition.
4. Return to Gleaner to read it. Refresh the page if needed.

Gleaner needs no API key. Your AI tool must be able to search the web and read and write local files. Charges depend on the AI tool or search service you use. [Choose an AI tool](/en/wiki/ai-workspace/).

## Read and update briefings

Use **简报** (briefing) to read the latest edition, **全部** (all items) to browse earlier items, and **收藏** (bookmarks) to revisit saved items. You can add notes, mark an item as uninteresting, or ask the AI to dig deeper or find similar items.

When you want an update, tell your AI to refresh the briefing. It gathers news for your current topics and handles any requests you left in Gleaner. You start each update. Change the scope in **主题** (topics) or in the AI chat. If you switch tools or lose the conversation, send the original handoff message again and ask the AI to continue with your saved topics.

Topics, briefings, bookmarks, feedback, and update history stay on this computer. You can read saved items and leave feedback while the AI is unavailable. Removing a topic keeps its past items. Before deleting the app, check the confirmation screen to see which files and data will be kept.
