# Readio

Bring in an English article and work through it one sentence at a time. Readio generates spoken audio, translates sentences, and looks up words. You can save material for review and pick up where you left off.

App links [GitHub](https://github.com/happypandaa/Readio.git) · [Gitee](https://gitee.com/mycolorfuldays/readio.git)

## Add and configure the app

In Panda Drawer, find Readio under **Recommended apps** and click **Add**. You can also paste one of the links above into **App link**. After adding the app, open **App configuration** and enter your keys.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| DeepSeek API key (required) | Translates sentences and looks up words. Create a key on the [DeepSeek platform](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys). |
| Fish Audio API key (required) | Generates spoken audio for each sentence. Create a key on [Fish Audio](https://fish.audio/app/api-keys/). |
| Fish Audio model (optional) | Leave this blank to use `s2.1-pro-free`. The default voice is Jessica. |

If the recommended app card mentions Doubao Voice, use the fields in **App configuration** after adding Readio. For an existing copy, follow the fields it actually shows. Translation, word lookup, and audio require an internet connection. Check with the providers for quotas and pricing. Save your settings, then open Readio.

## Read, listen, and save your progress

Try a built-in article first. To paste your own English text, click **添加文章** (add article). The toolbar for each sentence lets you play, translate, or save it. Select a word to look it up. Use **句子** (sentences) or **生词** (vocabulary) to review your saved items.

Articles, progress, and saved items are kept in browser data for the app window. Use the top menu to export a backup. Importing asks for confirmation before replacing your current data. Spoken audio needs to be generated again after importing. Export anything you want to keep before deleting the app.
