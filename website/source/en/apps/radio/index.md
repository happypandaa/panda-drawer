# Radio Bob

<img src="/images/bob.png" alt="Radio Bob pixel-art avatar" width="88" height="88">

Tell Bob which artist, style, or mood you want. It finds playable tracks and writes short introductions in Chinese. You can also search for tracks, edit the play queue, or save your music preferences.

App links [GitHub](https://github.com/happypandaa/radio-bob.git) · [Gitee](https://gitee.com/mycolorfuldays/radio-bob.git)

## Add the app

In Panda Drawer, find Radio Bob under **Recommended apps** and click **Add**. You can also paste one of the links above into **App link**. Wait for the app to finish adding before entering your settings.

## Setup

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| DeepSeek API key (required) | Selects songs and generates introductions. Create a key on the [DeepSeek platform](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys). |
| Fish API key (optional) | Turns written introductions into speech. Create a key on [Fish Audio](https://fish.audio/app/api-keys/). Music and written introductions work with this field blank. |

Save your keys under **App configuration** in the app details, then click **Open**. Song selection, speech, and music require an internet connection. For quotas and usage fees, see the service providers' own information.

## Search, then try a request

Start by searching for a familiar song. Try playing, pausing, and skipping it. Then ask Bob for something to relax to in the evening and try it without spoken introductions first.

Availability and playback length depend on the track and your account permissions. Some tracks are previews. For spoken introductions, enter a Fish API key and turn on **自动口播** (automatic narration).

Chat history and music preferences stay on your computer. Closing the Radio Bob window stops the station. You can pick up where you left off when you open it again.
