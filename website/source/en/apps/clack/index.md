# Clack

Choose a Chinese or English passage and type it line by line. Once you fill a line, Clack moves to the next one. At the end, you can review your speed and accuracy or practice the characters you got wrong.

Regular practice needs no AI tool, account, or API key. You can practice offline after adding Clack.

<h2 id="add-and-start-practicing">Add and start practicing</h2>

The screenshots show the Chinese interface. You can switch Clack to English with the **EN** button at the top right.

1. In Panda Drawer, click **Add application**. Find Clack under **Recommended apps**, then click **Add**. When you have no apps yet, the main screen also shows the recommendations.
2. If you see **Preparation needs confirmation**, click **Prepare and add** and wait. Then open Clack. It opens in its own window on **Practice**. If the recommendations say **All recommended apps have been added**, open Clack from **My apps**.

{% screenshot s02-add-app.webp 1440 810 Panda Drawer Add application panel in Chinese with recommendations below the app link %}

3. In **Practice**, pick a passage language and try **Short** for your first session. Check the suggested passage, then click **Start**. Choose **Another one** for a different suggestion, or choose a passage from the list below.

{% screenshot a01-clack-practice.webp 1440 746 Clack Quick start in Chinese showing language and length choices and the Start button %}

4. Type the text above the input box. Clack advances when you fill the line.

{% screenshot a03-clack-typing.webp 1440 746 Clack practice in Chinese with the passage above the input box %}

5. Review your result after you finish. Open **History** at the top of the window to find the passage you just completed.

{% screenshot a02-clack-history.webp 1440 740 Clack History in Chinese showing the completed passage A Cup of Hot Tea and its result %}

If the result is missing, select the language you practiced in **History**. Clack tracks Chinese and English separately. Your history and settings stay on your computer. You can export a backup from **History**.

If the download is slow in China, open **Settings**, find **Download source**, and choose **China (Gitee)**. You can also paste the [GitHub link](https://github.com/happypandaa/Clack.git) or [Gitee link](https://gitee.com/mycolorfuldays/clack.git) into **App link** in **Add application**.

## Change how practice works

If the passage is hard to read, follow the [Clack tutorial](/en/tutorials/remix-an-app/) to ask your AI to make the text bigger.
