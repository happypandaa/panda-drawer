# Download and system requirements

**Panda Drawer is currently an internal development version. Public installers are not available yet.** If someone invited you to try it, ask them for the development build for your computer and the installation instructions.

## System requirements

| System | Development build requirements |
| --- | --- |
| macOS | macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon, with the workspace on an APFS volume on a local or directly attached drive |
| Windows | Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 on x64 with WebView2 Evergreen, with the workspace on an internal NTFS drive |

macOS supports AI tool detection, one-click handoff to AI, actions in the app window's title bar, and workspace migration. On Windows, copy the prompt and open the project folder from the app details, then continue in your own AI tool.

## Recent changes

November 13, 2026

- Panda Drawer can switch between English and Chinese. Apps can manage their own language settings.
- Gleaner is now a recommended app. Use your own AI to gather news on the topics you choose.
