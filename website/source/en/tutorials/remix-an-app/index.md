# Make Clack's practice text bigger

If the text you follow in Clack is hard to read, ask your own AI tool to make it a little bigger. You need a tool that can edit files on your computer, such as Codex or WorkBuddy. The screenshots show the Chinese interface.

## 1. Add Clack

[Add and open Clack](/en/apps/clack/#add-and-start-practicing). If you have already added it, open it from **My apps** in Panda Drawer.

## 2. Finish one passage

In **Practice**, pick a passage language and **Short**, then click **Start**. Notice the size of the text above the typing box. After you finish, open **History** and make a note of the passage title so you can check it later.

{% screenshot a03-clack-typing.webp 1440 746 Clack practice in Chinese with the passage above the typing box %}

## 3. Open Clack in your AI tool

1. In Panda Drawer, open Clack's app details and find **Make it your own**. On macOS, choose your tool and click **Change with WorkBuddy** or **Change with Codex**. You can also click **Change it your way** at the top right of the Clack window.
2. Check that your AI tool opened Clack's project folder. If Panda Drawer filled in a message, review it and send it. Wait for the AI to describe Clack and ask what you want to change.

{% screenshot s05-clack-modify.webp 1040 454 Clack app details in Chinese showing the WorkBuddy and copy prompt buttons %}

On Windows, or when the AI tool did not open or receive a message, click **Only copy the prompt** in Clack's app details. Open the whole Clack folder listed in the message in your AI tool. Paste the message into a conversation that can edit files, then send it. On Windows, you can find the folder with **Open in File Explorer**. [More ways to connect your AI tool](/en/wiki/ai-workspace/).

## 4. Ask for one specific change

Send this in the same conversation:

```text
The passage above the typing box in Clack is hard to read.
Please make that text a little bigger. Keep regular practice and my existing history.
Tell me where to check the result when you finish.
```

If the AI suggests a bigger change, ask it to change only the text above the typing box for now.

## 5. Open the updated app

When the AI says it is done, finish any practice in progress and follow its instructions.

- If the AI asks you to prepare the app, close Clack, open its details, and click **Prepare app**. Wait for it to finish, then click **Open**.
- If the AI asks you to reload, click **Reload** at the top right of Clack on macOS. On Windows, expand **More** in the app details, open **Detailed runtime information**, and click **Reload project window**.
- If Clack is closed, click **Open** in its app details.

{% screenshot s06-reload.webp 474 66 Reload and Change it your way in the upper right of a macOS app window in Chinese %}

If you still see the old version, check the [troubleshooting guide](/en/wiki/faq/). If there is an error, give the exact message and the last button you clicked to the same AI.

## 6. Check the text and your history

Open the same passage again and see whether the text is easier to read. Type a few lines to check that practice still works. Then open **History** and look for the result you saved before the change.

For another change, you could ask for a 60-second practice mode that shows the result at the end and keeps regular practice and existing history.

## Before deleting Clack

Deleting a Clack installed from the recommendations also deletes its project folder and practice history. Export anything you need before deleting it and check the confirmation screen. If you are done for now, close the window.
